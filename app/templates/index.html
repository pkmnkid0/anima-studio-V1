Multi-resolution export

Makes smaller copies of every image in this folder (including subfolders) at the resolutions you pick below - lower-resolution training data trains noticeably faster. Captions are copied next to their images. Output goes to "resized images/<folder name> <resolution>" inside this folder - one folder per resolution, named after this folder (e.g. "my art 512" and "my art 1024"), with images and captions together. Subfolders keep their names inside. Your originals are never touched.
